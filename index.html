Top 10 Strongest Characters in Marvel Universe


  1. The One Above All - The ultimate god and creator of all existence in Marvel. They have absolute omnipotence, omniscience, and omnipresence.
  2. The Fulcrum - An enigmatic cosmic entity that acts as an extension or companion of the One-Above-All, overseeing the balance and the Jack Kirby-inspired cosmic order.
  3. The Beyonders / Pre-Retcon Beyonder - An ancient race of extra-dimensional beings who viewed the entire multiverse as a tiny science experiment, with power to destroy all realities effortlessly.
  4. The Living Tribunal - The cosmic judge of the multiverse. Second only to the One-Above-All, it maintains absolute balance across every dimension.
  5. Heart of the Universe Thanos - After Thanos absorbed this artifact, he became one with all of existence, easily surpassing the Living Tribunal and Eternity before resetting reality.
  6. God Emperor Doom - During Secret Wars (2015), Doctor Doom stole the power of the Beyonders to become the literal god of Battleworld, rewriting reality with a thought.
  7. Molecule Man (Owen Reese) - A living multiversal engine capable of controlling all matter and energy. His full power can unmake or recreate existence itself.
  8. Franklin Richards - The mutant son of Mr. Fantastic and the Invisible Woman. As an adult, his reality-warping powers allow him to dream up and create entire multiverses from scratch.
  9. The Phoenix Force - The primal universal embodiment of life, passion, and destruction. In its ultimate form (White Phoenix of the Crown), it can burn and rebirth entire realities.
  10. Eternity (with Infinity and Oblivion) - The abstract living embodiment of time and the universe itself, representing the total sum of all living things in existence.
